ABOUT UNN

Founded in 1955 by Dr. Nnamdi Azikiwe and officially opened in 1960, the University of Nigeria, Nsukka (UNN) is a premier, full-fledged federal university located in Enugu State, Nigeria.

Driven by its motto, "To Restore the Dignity of Man," UNN stands as the first autonomous university in post-independence Nigeria, pioneering a modern West African higher education model inspired by the American land-grant system.

Across its main campus in Nsukka, the Enugu Campus (UNEC), and the College of Medicine at Ituku-Ozalla, the university offers rigorous academic programs spanning the arts, humanities, engineering, social sciences, and medical sciences, maintaining its legacy as a top-tier hub for landmark research, technological innovation, and cultural heritage.

UNN Anthem

Under the leadership of Professor Simon Uchenna Ortuanya, who assumed office as the 16th Vice Chancellor of the University of Nigeria, Nsukka (UNN) in August 2025, the university's ongoing infrastructure revival is best described as an aggressive effort to restore neglected assets and modernize key services.

Key Renovation & Infrastructure Highlights

Road Network Rehabilitation:
Extensive repairs have been executed across major campus arteries, including Elias Avenue, Zik’s Drive, Chitis/Alumni Road, Main Gate Road, and the VC’s Office Road.
Commercial Services Restoration:
The long-abandoned UNN Filling Station was revived and brought back into full operation with newly installed modern pumps.
​Student Housing Improvement:
Systematic overhauls are underway across student residential quarters—including targeted hostel maintenance at both the Nsukka Main Campus (e.g., Balewa Hostel) and the Enugu Campus (Kwame Nkrumah Hostel)—alongside approvals for new specialized student housing.
Stalled Projects Reactivation:
Work resumed on the decade-long stalled 10-storey Senate Building project, pushing it past initial piling stages to active structural construction.
Power & Energy Upgrades:
Physical restoration of the VC's Lodge, along with dedicated power projects—such as a mini-power grid setup for the main university library and solar lighting installations at the Enugu campus.
Academic & Facility Expansion:
Restoration work on academic facilities, including the Food Science and Pharmacy buildings, accompanied by TETFund-backed mobilizations for new laboratory buildings and medical faculty blocks.

Overall, the administration's physical overhaul reflects a deliberate strategy to address chronic deferred maintenance, improve basic student living conditions, and stabilize essential utilities.

Established in 1961 as a College of Engineering, the Faculty of Engineering at the University of Nigeria, Nsukka (UNN) was the first fully established university engineering faculty in Nigeria. Over six decades, it has developed into one of West Africa's leading centers for technical education and applied research.

  • First Autonomous Nigerian University:
    • Formally opened on October 7, 1960—coinciding with Nigeria's independence—UNN became the first fully indigenous and degree-granting autonomous university in post-independence Nigeria.
  • Pioneered American Land-Grant Model:
    • Unlike older institutions modeled on the British system, UNN was the first university in Africa structured after the American land-grant university model (specifically through early partnerships with Michigan State University).
